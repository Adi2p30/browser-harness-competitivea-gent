Check your junk mail folder if you do not see the email. Log in using your birth date and this temporary PIN. Once logged in, you will be prompted to create a password. Use this password along with your email address in subsequent logins. Activate Your Account using your temporary PIN. Begin your application. Your application will autosave as you move between fields. Complete your application! Technical Questions? Visit the graduate admissions FAQ hub or contact gradinfo@purdue.edu .
