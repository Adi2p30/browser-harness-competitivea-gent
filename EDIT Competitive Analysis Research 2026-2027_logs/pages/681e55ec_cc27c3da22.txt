Tuition for online programs is generally lower than an in-person program, especially for out-of-state students. For Arizona residents, online tuition is less than in-person for the first 11 credit hours. For anything over 11 credit hours, online and in-person tuition is the same. Does ASU Online have out-of-state tuition? There is no out-of-state tuition for up to 11 credit hours each semester. At 11 credit hours, Arizona residents benefit from a tuition cap. This means your tuition will not increase with any additional credits you take. Out-of-state students donât receive a tuition cap, so taking a heavier course load may lead to higher overall tuition costs. How many transfer credits does ASU accept? We accept up to 64 credits from community, junior or two-year colleges. There is no set limit on the credits we accept from four-year colleges and universities. But, you must complete 30 credits at ASU to graduate from your degree program. Before applying your credits towards your degree, our team must evaluate your transfer credits. This happens after you apply. Learn more about our transfer credits . What are the fees included with my tuition estimate? ASU tuition and fees are approved annually by the Arizona Board of Regents. Federal aid : Federal aid can be need-based or non-need-based. Both are dependent on your income, background and other qualifications. You must be a U.S. citizen or an eligible non-citizen and submit the FAFSA (Free Application for Federal Student Aid) every year to receive federal aid. Grants : Grants are need-based aid that donât require repayment. Learn more about student grants . Loans : Subsidized (need-based) and unsubsidized (non-need-based) loans are money you borrow and must pay back with interest. Learn more about loans . Scholarships : Another form of free financial aid, scholarships may be need-based or merit-based. Additionally, ASU offers scholarships for eligible online students . Once admitted to ASU, submit your FAFSA and you'll automatically be considered based on your eligibility. No separate scholarship application required.
