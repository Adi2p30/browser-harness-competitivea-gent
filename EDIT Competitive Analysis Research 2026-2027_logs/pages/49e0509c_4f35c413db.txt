Our students bring their academic, professional, and personal experiences to the classroom in fields like operations, business management, finance, program management, and marketing. Industry experiences span areas like healthcare, aerospace, transportation, retail, food, military, and manufacturing. Whether on campus or online, you’ll tackle real-world assignments that will prepare you to align business processes, mitigate supply chain risks, increase supply chain agility, and deliver a competitive advantage for your organization.
